## Demo description

If you want to enable switching between week & month view, you can do it by adding a segmented control to the header and dynamically change the `calendarType` option. If you are not looking for dynamic switching you can configure a [week calendar](https://demo.mobiscroll.com/react/calendar/week-view#) or a [month calendar](https://demo.mobiscroll.com/react/calendar/multiple-months#).

## What this demo shows

- A date picker calendar with a view switcher for changing between week and month views.
- **Header and segmented control** A centered segmented control provides icon-based week and month options. It sits between the month and year quick navigation label on the left and the previous and next navigation arrows on the right.
- **Week view** The week view is selected by default and displays the current week below the header.
- **Month view** Selecting the month option displays a full month. A fixed weekday header shows abbreviated day names from Sunday through Saturday, with dates arranged in a grid below.
- **Navigation** Drag the calendar left or right to move between months.
- **Day cell states** Hovering over a day highlights its number with a gray background. Selecting a day highlights its number with a blue background. The current date is highlighted by default and remains blue when another date is selected.
- **Adjacent months** Dates from the previous and next months use a muted style. Selecting one navigates the calendar to the corresponding month.

## Best for

- **Appointment booking** Let users focus on dates in the current week or switch to a month view to compare availability across a longer period.
- **Travel and accommodation date selection** Support quick near-term date choices while allowing users to scan dates across the full month.
- **Delivery and service scheduling** Help users choose an upcoming day in week view or review a broader set of available dates in month view.
- **Responsive date selection** Provide both a compact weekly layout and a broader monthly overview from the same calendar header.
